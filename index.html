What you're looking at

Driver: the vocal folds (the flaps in the larynx) chop the airstream into a buzz at the fundamental frequency. That buzz is rich in harmonics.

Resonators: each cavity and bony structure has its own preferred frequency. They respond strongly when the fundamental — or one of its harmonics — lands near their natural mode.

Coupling: the vagus nerve (green path winding through the chest) and the body's tissues mechanically and neurologically link these zones. Even sub-threshold vibration in one zone biases the others — the "tuning" case.

Whole-system mode: at certain fundamentals, multiple resonators light up simultaneously through harmonic stacking. That's the singer's experience of the note "ringing through everything."

Anti-resonance: at the geometric mean between adjacent zone pairs (the ◊-marked buttons on the left), coupled oscillators interfere destructively. Response drops below baseline — not silence, but active cancellation.
