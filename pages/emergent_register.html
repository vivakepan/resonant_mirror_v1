Emergent register — the hysteresis loop is measured, not asserted

Two competing limit-cycle oscillators (src/register_dynamics.js) are integrated while the sung pitch is swept up, then down. The register is not a stored label — it is which oscillator wins. The ascending and descending paths do not retrace: the enclosed area is the passaggio barrier, and it emerges from the dynamics rather than from an if.

chest cycle dominant head cycle dominant bistable band (both stable — history decides)
up-break — down-break — bistable width —

Illustrative, not a measurement. The oscillator coefficients are chosen, not measured from any body (they are free parameters — see DYN_P and AIN-RS-016). What is grounded is that hysteresis emerges: raise Effort and watch the ascending break climb until, at full effort, the head branch becomes unreachable across the whole range — the barrier cannot be out-run by pushing. Then drop effort and it becomes reachable again. That is crossing by subtraction. This page does not listen to you, does not measure your body, and does not tell you whether you sang well.
